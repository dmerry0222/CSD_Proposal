WBL Course Section and Cost Planner

Projects how many sections of the work-based learning course each department needs per term, and what that costs, under three ways of grouping students. Option 2 is the one you tune: combine departments within a college and watch the cost move between the two bookends.

Tunable Variables

Departments

  • You can manually change, by department, the number of students on WBL each term
    (this updates counts across all 3 options shown above)
  • Group departments' sections to adjust the cost of Option 2 above

How a section count is reached. Students per department are the department's share of the class year, rounded up. Each term's students are divided by the seats per section and rounded up, so a department with four students in a term still consumes a whole section. That rounding is the entire reason the three options differ: Option 1 pays it once per department per term, Option 3 pays it once per college per term, and Option 2 pays it once per group you define.

Where the waste is. A department whose Option 2 sections hold far more seats than it has students is carrying a nearly empty section, and those are the ones worth combining. Chemistry and Env Science open combined as a worked example; separate them to see what splitting them costs. The Excel copy carries a fill percentage per department for sorting.
